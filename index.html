ViralClip AI

A self-hosted tool that finds the clippable moments in long YouTube and Twitch videos, cuts them into vertical clips, and uploads them to your own YouTube channel.

What it does

  1. You point it at a YouTube video or a Twitch VOD.
  2. It transcribes the audio locally, and on Twitch also reads the chat replay to find the moments people reacted to.
  3. It suggests clips with a timestamp, a score and a reason for each.
  4. You review them, keep the ones you like and drop the rest.
  5. It cuts the keepers to vertical, writes a title and description, and uploads them to your YouTube channel.

It learns from your keeps and drops over time — both which videos tend to be worth clipping and what a good moment looks like on your channel.

Who it is for

One person: the operator running it. ViralClip AI is not a hosted service and has no public sign-up. It runs on the operator's own computer, uploads only to the operator's own YouTube channel, and has no other users.

Why it asks for YouTube access

Connecting a YouTube account grants two permissions, and they are used for exactly two things:

  • youtube.upload — to upload finished clips to your own channel. Nothing is ever uploaded without you approving that clip first.
  • youtube.readonly — to read the view, like and comment counts on clips it uploaded, so it can tell which ones did well.

It never reads anyone else's private data, never changes your channel settings, and never deletes anything.

Where your data lives

On your own machine. The videos, the clips, the database and the access tokens all stay on the computer running the app — there is no ViralClip AI server to send them to. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is stored and the one case where text leaves your computer.

Source

Python and FastAPI on the backend, Next.js on the front, ffmpeg for the cutting, and faster-whisper for the transcription — all running locally.
