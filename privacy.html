Privacy Policy

ViralClip AI · last updated 18 September 2026

ViralClip AI is a self-hosted tool. It runs on the operator's own computer and there is no ViralClip AI server, no hosted account and no other user. This policy describes what the software stores on that computer and the specific cases where data is sent to a third party.

What is stored, and where

Everything below is stored locally on the computer running the app. None of it is transmitted to the developer, and there is no remote backup.

  • Google account access. The access and refresh tokens issued when you connect a YouTube account, kept in a local database and encrypted at rest.
  • Your channel name. Read once when you connect, so the app can show you which account is connected.
  • Video and clip files. Sources downloaded for processing and the clips cut from them, in a folder on the local disk.
  • Transcripts and metadata. Titles, creators, durations, view counts, transcripts and Twitch chat activity for the videos you process.
  • Your own decisions. Which clips you kept or dropped, and the view, like and comment counts of clips already uploaded — this is what the app learns from.

What leaves your computer

Three destinations, and nothing else.

  • Anthropic — video transcripts, titles and chat summaries are sent to the Claude API so it can suggest which moments to clip and write titles and descriptions. This is the one case where the content of your videos leaves the machine. Anthropic's handling of that text is governed by their own terms; it is not used to train models on API traffic.
  • Google / YouTube — the clip file, its title and description on upload, and requests for the view counts of clips already uploaded.
  • Twitch and YouTube, as a viewer — ordinary public requests to fetch a video, its metadata and its chat replay.

There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party script of any kind. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

How Google user data is used

ViralClip AI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • youtube.upload is used only to upload clips you have approved to your own channel.
  • youtube.readonly is used only to read the public statistics of videos the app itself uploaded, and your channel name at connection time.
  • Google user data is never transferred to anyone, never used for advertising, and never read by a human.
  • Google user data is not sent to the Claude API. What is sent there is video transcripts and titles, which come from the video files themselves.

Deleting your data, and revoking access

  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens immediately.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions page, which works whether or not the app is running.
  • Deleting the app's folder and its database removes everything else. There is no copy anywhere for the developer to delete on your behalf.

Children

ViralClip AI is a tool for a channel operator and is not directed at children under 13. It collects nothing from anyone other than the person running it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Because the software is self-hosted, an older installed copy keeps behaving the way it did when it was installed.
